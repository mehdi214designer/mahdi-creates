Fluent Forms: High-Performance Design for the Fastest Form Builder

A comprehensive web redesign for a market-leading WordPress plugin, focusing on showcasing lightning-fast performance and an intuitive "no-code" builder experience for 300,000+ businesses.

Performance Optimization
UI/UX Strategy
Services
Product Marketing & Web Redesign
Category
Marketing Infrastructure
Client
Fluent Forms (WPManageNinja)

Communicating Speed in a Cluttered Market

Fluent Forms is fundamentally faster and leaner than its competitors. However, the previous website didn't clearly communicate this technical advantage. The goal was to align the brand's visual speed with its actual performance.

Showcasing the "No-Code" Power

We wanted to prove that "powerful" doesn't have to mean "complex." The design needed to highlight the drag-and-drop simplicity while appealing to advanced developers who value clean code.

Bridging the Feature Gap

With 45+ integrations and advanced features like conversational forms, the site needed an organized architecture that allowed users to discover high-level features without feeling overwhelmed.

A Design Built on Lightweight Logic

I approached the redesign by treating the website like the plugin itself: lightweight, modular, and extremely fast. Every visual element was chosen to guide the user toward the Live Demo.

Interactive Feature Storytelling

I replaced long text descriptions with interactive "Feature Cards" and video snippets. This allowed potential users to feel the responsiveness of the UI before they even installed the plugin.

Optimizing the Conversion Funnel

We faced the challenge of a high bounce rate on the pricing page. I redesigned the pricing tiers to focus on "Value per Feature," making it easier for agencies and small businesses to choose the right plan.

Setting the Benchmark for WordPress Marketing

The new Fluent Forms site became a gold standard for the WPManageNinja ecosystem, resulting in higher time-on-page and a notable increase in "Free to Pro" conversions.

40% Increase in Demo Engagement

By making the "Try Live" action the centerpiece of the design, we saw a massive jump in users interacting with the product, which is the strongest lead-generation signal.

A Scalable Design Language

The project delivered a flexible UI kit that the marketing team now uses to launch new feature landing pages in hours rather than days, ensuring 100% brand consistency.
