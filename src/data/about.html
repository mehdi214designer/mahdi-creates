How I Got Here

2014

I touched Photoshop and Illustrator for the first time. I didn't even know "design" was a real job yet.

2015

I failed a board exam. People teased me for it, a lot, and it hurt. But that failure gave me something I still use today: I stopped being afraid of messing up in public. That's my superpower now.

I started in a print shop for a year, learning the basics. Then three years at a digital marketing agency, learning fast. Then I took a pay cut to join a second agency for a year and a half, because they taught me more than the last one paid me. I choose knowledge over money, every time.


That decision led me into software, first as a graphic designer, then as a UI designer. A year later, WPManageNinja gave me the Rising UI Designer Award.


I never finished my Business Administration degree. Covid hit halfway through, and life happened. I'm not going to pretend otherwise. Everything else on this page, I built without a finished degree, just by doing the work and refusing to quit.
